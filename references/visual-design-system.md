# Visual design system

Read styles.md for the six role mappings. One recognisable silhouette, one accent assembly, purposeful small parts and one consistent camera define the finish. No gradient materials, bloom or arbitrary stacks of slabs.

Top faces are lightest; screen-left sides are lit and screen-right sides shaded. Use rounded caps/joins and 0.5, 1 or 1.5 px outlines at display size. Interior marks are subordinate to the silhouette.

Icons read at 48 px. Scenes remain interesting at 4× zoom without broken joints. Anatomy generates its own sage plinth, ticks and label plate; do not add another decorative platform unless it belongs to the object.

UI text becomes rounded bars, fields and controls. Keep readable labels outside, apart from at most one tiny engraving. Review silhouette, proportion and occlusion before shade, colour, detail and motion. Reference images set mood, not permission to reproduce proprietary art.
