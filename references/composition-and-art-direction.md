# Composition and art direction

State what the picture shows and where it lives before modelling. A login card, server rack, control panel or laptop is clearer than an abstract arrangement of slabs.

Use one focal accent assembly, medium-detail supporting objects and quiet context. Mix one large, several medium and purposeful small parts. Leave intentional negative space; not every surface needs decoration. Keep line contrast subordinate inside the silhouette.

For a hero, leave room for neighbouring copy. For a card, frame tightly with equal padding. On mobile, simplify secondary groups rather than shrinking essential controls into illegibility. Anatomy supplies the base and ruler automatically.

Choose one of Stroke, Two-tone, Duotone, Fill, Anatomy or Yours. Default to Two-tone with the host brand accent. Material styles, neon glows and clay rendering are not part of this skill's v2 visual system. References set mood and finish, not copied geometry.
